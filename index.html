About EJT IND'L

EJT IND'L PAINTS AND SERVICES CO. is a duly registered business entity with the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI), specializing in trading, industrial painting, waterproofing, and technical industrial services.

We provide installation, maintenance, repair, rehabilitation, and performance optimization of pumps, motors, fire pump systems, and industrial coating systems. Our commitment is to deliver reliable, cost-effective, and high-quality solutions that exceed client expectations while maintaining safety, professionalism, and long-term value.
